## Notes

- The 2017 circular is marked "Withdrawn": its content was consolidated into the RBC Directions,
  2025 (Section D, paras 64-76), which applied from issuance on 28 Nov 2025 to commercial banks
  other than Small Finance Banks, Payments Banks, Regional Rural Banks and Local Area Banks.
- The 2026 Third Amendment Directions apply to transactions on or after 1 January 2027
  (para 3(2)), and only to commercial banks other than Small Finance Banks, Payments Banks,
  Regional Rural Banks and Local Area Banks.
